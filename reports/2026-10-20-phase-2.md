# Maison Academy · Phase 2 report

**Date:** 20 October 2026
**Phase:** 2, staff app
**Release:** `phase-2`

## What was built

**The staff app**, on the front page of https://maison-academy.netlify.app. It's still private to the Netlify team until Phase 4.

- **Activation:** staff enter a single-use code from their manager (or scan its QR code), read and accept the privacy notice, and choose a 6-digit PIN.
- **Login:** staff number and PIN. A phone stays logged in for 60 days.
- **Home:**
  - today's set of 5 cards
  - the weekly rhythm (sets this week against the target, and weeks in a row)
  - the person's level and what's needed for the next one
  - the venue's team goal
  - "Review my misses"
- **Answering:** one tap. Cards with several right answers, or steps to put in order, have a single Check button.
- **After each answer:** a green bar for "Got it" or a red bar for "Not yet", with the right answer, the reason, and why it matters on the floor. "Not yet" cards come back in the next set.
- **Set done:** new things learned, cards strengthened, the rhythm and the team goal. There's no score.
- **My progress:** the steps to the next level, a bar per track, and one next focus.
- **Readiness check:** 20 Level 1 cards, untimed. The result is "Ready" or "Not yet, here's what to practise", and it can be retaken any time.
- **Install help** for iPhone and Android, and a privacy page.
- **Low signal:** today's set is kept on the phone. Answers are saved and sent when the phone is back online, and each one counts only once.
- **Test accounts:** they get a "Next day" button to watch the spacing work, and a "Reset" button.

**Behind it:**

- **Spacing in the database:** the database grades every answer and moves cards between review gaps (next set, 1, 3, 7, 21 and 45 days). Allergen cards never go beyond 21 days.
- **Login security:** a small login service. It refuses obvious PINs, pauses login after 5 mistyped PINs, never says whether a staff number exists, and stores PINs only as a secure hash.
- **Studio > Staff:** add test accounts and print activation codes with a QR code. Real staff come in Phase 4, from the roster.

## Decisions made

1. **Defaults until confirmed:** 4 sets a week; the brief's Level 1 rules; English only; the working name "Maison Academy". All of these are settings that can change without code.
2. **Daily sets:** one set of 5 cards a day. Missed cards always come back in the next set.
3. **One tap to answer:** there's no "How sure were you?" step.
   - A card counts as known once it's been answered right three times in a row, spaced out.
   - This was David's call. The cost is that the private "sure but not right" allergen alert for managers can't happen.
4. **Result colours:** a green bar for "Got it" and a red bar for "Not yet" on the answer page. The page itself stays cream.
5. **My progress:** shows only level steps, tracks and next focus.
6. **Accounts before Phase 4:** only test accounts can be created.
7. **Privacy notice:** shown at activation as a draft, until counsel confirms the wording.

## Testing

- **On a copy of the database:** three test accounts practised over two simulated weeks.
  - Missed cards came back in the next set.
  - Known cards spaced out.
  - Allergen cards stopped at 21 days.
  - An answer sent twice counted once.
- **Privacy:** a staff account couldn't read anyone else's records, PIN details or activation codes. A venue manager could only see their own venue.
- **On the live database:** a three-day test in a throwaway account gave the same results. It was undone afterwards.
- **On a phone-sized screen:** no sideways scrolling, buttons at least 44 px, and none of the words the brief rules out about people.
- **David's test:** iPhone and Android, 20 October. Works well.
