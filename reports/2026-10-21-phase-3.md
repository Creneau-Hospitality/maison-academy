# Maison Academy · Phase 3 report

**Dates:** 20 to 21 October 2026
**Phase:** 3, manager dashboard
**Release:** `phase-3`

## What was built

**The manager dashboard**, in the studio under Dashboard. It's for the admin and venue managers.

- **Overview.** Headline figures for this week:
  - practised this week
  - weekly target met
  - floor ready (Level 1 or above)
  - average mastery
  - "new on the menu" progress

  Below them:
  - **Mastery grid:** by venue and track.
  - **Weekly chart:** target met, over the last 8 full weeks.
  - **Level mix:** where each team sits on the path.
  - **Coach this week:** the three weakest topics, each with a huddle question and its answer.
  - **Recognise:** most improved, longest run on target, and level-ups.
- **Team.** One row per person: level, weeks on target, this week's sets, progress per track, last active and next focus. It downloads as CSV.
- **Person.**
  - progress per track
  - the last four weeks, day by day
  - levels reached
  - practical sign-offs (table set-up, welcome and seating, allergen walk-through), signed from here; signing can move someone up a level, and undoing never moves anyone down
  - a leave week, one a month, so a holiday doesn't break a person's run
- **Questions.**
  - the most-missed cards, with first-time-right percentage and what people picked instead
  - cards everyone knows
  - the card bank
  - progress on "new on the menu"
- **New on the menu.** The admin picks the cards about new dishes. Staff get them first in their next sets until done.
- **Venue managers.** They sign in to the studio and see only Dashboard and Staff, for their own venue.
- **Demo venue.** 12 sample people (test accounts) with five weeks of practice, so the dashboard can be tried before real staff. Test and sample accounts never count in real figures unless asked for. The demo venue is removed with one command before the pilot.

## Decisions made

1. **Dashboard rules (defaults):**
   - **Coach this week:** the units with the lowest share of right answers over the last 4 weeks.
   - **Most improved:** compares average mastery today with 4 weeks ago.
   - **Weekly chart:** shows full weeks only.
   - **Questions view:** a card shows once 10 people have answered it; "everyone knows" means 95% right first time.
2. **Venue managers:** they can sign off practicals and mark leave weeks. Only the admin makes "new on the menu" sets.
3. **Demo venue:** loaded for trying the dashboard, and removed before the pilot.
