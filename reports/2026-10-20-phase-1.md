# Maison Academy · Phase 1 report

**Dates:** 16 to 20 October 2026
**Phase:** 1, card bank and content studio
**Release:** `phase-1`

## What was built

- **The content studio** at https://maison-academy.netlify.app/studio, for David and the content reviewers:
  - the card bank, with counts and filters
  - a card editor with a preview of what staff see
  - approve, send back, retire and full version history
  - the kitchen check for allergen cards
  - import and export (JSON and CSV), people and roles, and the learning settings
- **The database rules** in Supabase (Frankfurt). The database itself, not just the screen, decides who can read or change what:
  - Only admins and content reviewers can see cards.
  - Editing a card's content saves a new version and sends it back to review.
  - A card can only go live with a named reviewer.
  - An allergen card can only go live after a recorded kitchen check.
  - Approvals, retirements, imports, exports and role changes are written to an audit log.
- **Answer order:** the options appear in a new random order every time, so the right answer isn't always first.

## Cards added and improved

- **Harder wrong options** on 122 cards (a sample of 10, then 112 more). They use the same questions, facts and right answers. The wrong options now come from our own menus and standards, so they sound right.
- **21 sequence-of-service cards** from the 11-step service document. The document is also saved as its own training document (`2. TRAINING/Sequence of Service/SOS_Service_Steps`).
- **40 selling cards** at Level 2, from the Server Training Guide: Sales Techniques:
  - suggestive selling (15)
  - reading the guest (11)
  - upselling (14)

  The examples use real Arabian Ranches dishes and prices.

## Decisions made

1. Phase 1 went ahead without waiting for Gerard. His owner invitations stay open.
2. Allergen cards stay hidden from staff until someone from the kitchen has checked them. That can be the Arabian Ranches head chef, working from a one-page list.
3. Voids always need a manager's authorisation.
4. **Check-backs:** within 2 minutes of the food being served. Naming the dish ("How is your steak?") is preferred; "How are you enjoying your meal?" is also accepted.
5. **The bill:** present it within 2 to 3 minutes of the request, with payment done within 5.
6. **Selling cards:** they use our menu rather than the guide's generic examples.

The full list is in `DECISIONS.md`.
