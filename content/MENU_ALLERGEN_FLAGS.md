# Allergen flags from the Arabian Ranches menus

**For:** the head chef, as the allergen check.
**Checked:** 16 October 2026.
**Menus:** breakfast `20260914_MM-AR-BREAKFAST53.pdf` and lunch and dinner `20260922_MM-AR-LUNCHDINNER39.pdf`.

These flags come only from the ingredients the menu itself lists. They are not a full allergen check, because recipes can hold allergens the menu doesn't mention (in butter, sauces, dressings, batters, stocks and so on). Please confirm or correct each one.

## Why the menu can't be the allergen source

- **Allergens covered.** The menus mark only nuts (N) and shellfish (S), plus a gluten-free claim (GF). The other allergen groups are not marked at all: gluten, milk, egg, fish, crustaceans, molluscs, sesame, soy, mustard, celery, lupin and sulphites.
- **Conflict with the training.** The Service Training Program and the Operations Manual both say "All 14 major allergens are identified on the menu". They aren't, so staff taught that line may rely on the menu when they should ask the kitchen.

## Codes and print points

- **(SE) and (D).** The Lamb Chops carry (SE) and the Mediterranean Power Bowl carries (D). Neither code is in the legend.
- **Breakfast legend.** It doesn't define (GF), (S) or (R), but the page uses (GF) and (S).
- **Editing note.** The Crab Omelet description begins with "Change description :", which looks like a note left in by mistake.
- **Spellings.** These matter because cards copy the printed spelling:
  - "Cordon Blue Deluxe"
  - "Mozarella"
  - "Alchol" in the legend
  - "parlsey"
  - "carmelized"

## What the cards will do until the matrix arrives

- Food cards teach what the menu states: components, dietary marks, what each code means, and which dishes are raw.
- "Contains" allergen cards are drafted only from listed ingredients, marked [TO CONFIRM]. None goes live without the kitchen check.
- No card says a dish is free from any allergen.
- The allergen SOP cards teach "ask the head chef, every time", not "check the menu".
